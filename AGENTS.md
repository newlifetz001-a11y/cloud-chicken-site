# Cloud Chicken Website Project Rules

- This is a static HTML/CSS/JS restaurant website.
- Do not add WordPress, React, Vue, Next.js, Tailwind, Bootstrap, or any CMS.
- Keep noindex,nofollow until the final production domain is ready.
- Do not invent phone numbers, prices, business hours, reviews, awards, or fake links.
- Preserve the Cloud Chicken brand style: sky blue, orange checkerboard, rounded buttons, clouds, warm family-friendly feeling.
- Check desktop at 1280px width.
- Check mobile at 390px width.
- Check all buttons: Order Online, View Menu, Get Directions, Menu, Family Meals, About, Visit Us.
- After every task, summarize files changed, problems fixed, owner information needed, and deployment readiness.
